Concept of the day

Beta vs alpha: was it skill or the market?
Beta is the part of a return that came from the market moving; alpha is what's left after that. If the whole market rose 5% and your stock rose 6%, most of the gain was beta. This is why our scorecard grades calls on excess return versus a benchmark, not raw price moves.
