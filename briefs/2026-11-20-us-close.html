Concept of the day

Earnings: beating expectations isn't enough
Share prices react to results versus what analysts expected, and even more to guidance — what the company says about the next quarters. A company can beat on profit and still fall if guidance is weak, because the price already reflected the good quarter.

Today: The story shows that even though Nvidia had strong earnings, investors remained cautious, highlighting that beating expectations isn't enough to guarantee positive market reactions.
