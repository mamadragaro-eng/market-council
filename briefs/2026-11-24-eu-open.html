Concept of the day

Diversification: the only free lunch
Holding assets that don't move together (different sectors, regions, bonds, cash) lowers the swings of the whole portfolio without necessarily lowering expected return. Owning ten tech stocks is not diversified: they tend to fall together.

Today: The story highlights how heavily Nvidia's stock depends on one individual, Jensen Huang, which shows the risks of not diversifying investments in single stocks or companies.
