The council's calls

Calls come from a decision board: several AI members, each looking at the market from a different angle, vote independently. The board's call is worked out from their votes, and members with a better track record count for more. The question for every instrument: will it beat its benchmark (S&P 500 for US names, STOXX Europe 600 for European ones) over the horizon? How it works.
