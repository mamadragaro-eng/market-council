Every council call is graded once its horizon has passed: did the instrument beat its benchmark by more than half its usual volatility (Bullish), lag it by that much (Bearish), or stay within the band (Neutral)? Hit rates come with 95% intervals and are compared with simple baselines. Nothing is deleted: calls that must not count are listed below with the reason.
