Scorecard

Every council call is graded once its horizon has passed: did the instrument beat its benchmark by more than half its usual volatility (Bullish), lag it by that much (Bearish), or stay within the band (Neutral)? Hit rates come with 95% intervals and are compared with simple baselines. Nothing is deleted: test runs that never counted are listed on the How it works page, with proof the record hasn't been edited.

Tap ☆ to keep an instrument on top. Favourites are saved in this browser only.
